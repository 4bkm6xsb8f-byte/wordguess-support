How to play

Guess the five-letter word in six tries. After each guess, every tile's color and shape mark show how close you were: a check mark means the letter is in the right spot, a triangle means it is in the word but in a different spot, and a dash means it isn't in the word at all.

Frequently asked

Is there a daily puzzle? Yes. Daily gives everyone the same word each day (puzzle #1 was 1 January 2026, and a new one arrives on your device's local calendar day) and tracks a day streak. Unlimited deals a fresh word whenever you want, with no repeats among your recent games.

How do day streaks work? Solve the daily puzzle on its day and your streak goes up by one. Miss a day and it resets. Unlimited rounds don't count toward it.

Does WordGuess collect my data? No. There are no accounts, no ads, and no tracking, and nothing is sent to FH Development Studio. See our Privacy Policy.

Where are my stats kept, and do they move between devices? Your game history and stats are stored on your device. If you're signed in to iCloud, they also sync through your own private iCloud account, so they show up on your other devices signed in to the same Apple Account and come back if you reinstall the app. We can't see that data. With iCloud off, everything stays on the device and the game works the same. Hard Mode, reminders, theme, and background style sync the same way through iCloud; a background photo stays on the device it was chosen on.

How do I turn on Hard Mode? Open Settings from the game screen and toggle Hard Mode. It applies straight away, including to a round already in progress.
