How to play

Guess the five-letter word in six tries. After each guess, every tile's color and shape mark shows how close you were: a filled tile in the right spot, a filled tile in the wrong spot, or an empty tile if the letter isn't in the word at all.

Frequently asked

Is there a daily puzzle? Not yet — WordGuess currently offers unlimited rounds with a fresh word each time.

Does WordGuess collect my data? No. There are no accounts, no ads, and no tracking, and nothing is sent to FH Development Studio. See our Privacy Policy.

Where are my stats kept, and do they move between devices? Your game history and stats are stored on your device. If you're signed in to iCloud, they also sync through your own private iCloud account, so they show up on your other devices signed in to the same Apple Account and come back if you reinstall the app. We can't see that data. With iCloud off, everything stays on the device and the game works the same. Your settings — Hard Mode, theme, background — stay on the device and don't sync.

How do I turn on Hard Mode? Open Settings from the game screen and toggle Hard Mode. It takes effect on your next new game.
