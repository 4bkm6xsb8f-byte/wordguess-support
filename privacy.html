FH WordGuess

Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 27, 2026

FH Development Studio ("we," "us") built WordGuess as a word game with no accounts and no servers of our own. This policy is short because the app does very little with data. It also covers this website, which hosts the support pages, the browser version of the daily puzzle, and the pages that challenge links open.

What we collect

Nothing. WordGuess has no user accounts, includes no analytics, advertising, or tracking SDKs, and sends nothing to FH Development Studio. We operate no servers for this app, so there is nothing for us to receive.

What's stored on your device

Your game history and statistics (when you played, which puzzle it was, the word, your guesses, whether you won, how many tries it took, and whether Hard Mode was on) are stored on your device using Apple's SwiftData framework. Your preferences (Hard Mode, reminders, theme, background style, and the mode you last played) and your daily-puzzle progress are stored on your device too; a background photo you choose stays on the device it was chosen on. The app also remembers that you have seen the introduction and whether it has already offered reminders; both are kept with your preferences. The Home Screen and Lock Screen widgets and the iMessage app read a small snapshot of today's puzzle and your streak from a container shared with them on the same device.

iCloud sync

If you're signed in to iCloud, your game history and statistics also sync through your own private iCloud account, using Apple's CloudKit, and your preferences and daily-puzzle progress sync through iCloud's key-value storage. That means they reach your other devices signed in to the same Apple Account, and they come back if you reinstall the app. This data:

  • Lives in your private iCloud database, under your own Apple Account
  • Is not visible or accessible to FH Development Studio, and we have no way to read it
  • Is never sent to any server run by us or by any third party
  • Is handled by Apple under Apple's own iCloud privacy practices, not ours

If you're not signed in to iCloud, or iCloud is unavailable for the app, everything stays on the device and the game works exactly the same — sync is the only thing you lose.

Deleting the app removes the copy on that device. Removing the data held in your iCloud account is done through Apple's own iCloud settings, since it's your account, not ours.

Game Center

Leaderboards and achievements are optional and use your own Game Center account. Scores and achievements you post are held by Apple under Apple's own privacy terms. If you're not signed in to Game Center, you can play the whole game without posting anything. When you are signed in, the app also reads your own streak and win numbers back from Game Center and keeps a copy on the device, so a streak carries on after a reinstall or on a new device; it reads no one else's scores.

Reminders

Daily reminders are off until you turn them on. They are local notifications scheduled on your device at the time you choose; no push service or server is involved.

Contacts and photos

Invite Friends lets you pick people from your contacts. The picker is Apple's own and runs outside the app: WordGuess never asks for access to your contacts, is given only the people you tap, and reads only their names, phone numbers, and email addresses. It uses them to address the text or email you are about to send in Apple's own Messages or Mail composer, and it neither stores them nor sends them anywhere itself. Choosing a background photo works the same way: Apple's photo picker hands the app only the photo you choose, and that photo is kept on that device.

Challenge links

When you share a puzzle or challenge a friend, the app builds a link and hands it to the share sheet; where it goes from there is up to you. The link holds the puzzle's number (or, for an Unlimited round, the code that recreates that round) and, if you had finished the puzzle, how many tries it took you. It holds no name, account, or device identifier, and we are not told that you shared it.

This website

The site has no accounts, no cookies, no analytics, and no advertising, and we keep no record of who visits it. Three things happen when you use it:

  • Hosting. The pages are served by GitHub Pages. Like any web host, GitHub receives your IP address and the address of the page you asked for, and may log them for security. We have no access to those logs. GitHub handles them under its own privacy statement.
  • Fonts. The puzzle, hints, past puzzles, and press pages load the Barlow typeface from Google Fonts, so your browser also contacts Google, which receives your IP address and browser details in the course of sending the font files. This page and the support page load nothing from Google.
  • Your guesses in the browser puzzle. If you play a daily puzzle on the site, your guesses for that puzzle are saved in your browser's local storage so the board is still there when you come back. They never leave your browser, we cannot read them, and clearing the site's data in your browser settings removes them.

A challenge link opened in a browser shows the sender's result from the link itself. Nothing about the person opening it is recorded.

Third parties

The WordGuess app uses no third-party SDKs, analytics services, or advertising networks of any kind. The only outside services involved in the app are Apple's iCloud and, if you use it, Game Center, both acting on your account. The website involves GitHub, which hosts it, and Google Fonts, as described above.

Children's privacy

WordGuess does not collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13, for the same reason stated above: neither the app nor this website collects data from anyone.
