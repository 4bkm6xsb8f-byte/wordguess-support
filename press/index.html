What makes it different

  • Challenge a friend. Share a spoiler-free card of your grid; the link opens the very same puzzle for whoever taps it, in the app or on the App Store first.
  • Built-in accessibility. Every tile state carries its own shape mark alongside its color, from day one rather than as a separate mode. VoiceOver labels on every tile and key, and Dynamic Type support.
  • A daily and an unlimited mode. The same word for everyone each day, with day streaks and a countdown, plus a fresh word whenever you like.
  • Past Puzzles. Any earlier daily can be played later; archive rounds never touch the day streak.
  • Game Center. Leaderboards for streaks and wins, seven achievements.
  • iMessage app. Send today's result or a challenge straight from Messages.
  • Streak reminders. An optional, on-device nudge at a time you choose. No server, no push account.
  • Learn the word. A definition appears after every round.
  • Syncs quietly. History and settings follow you across devices through iCloud.

Boilerplate

FH Word Guess is a free daily word game for iPhone and iPad from FH Development Studio. Guess the five-letter word in six tries, keep a streak going, and challenge friends with a link that opens the exact puzzle you played. It has no ads, no tracking and no account, and its shape-marked tiles make it playable without relying on color.
