About the daily puzzle

WordGuess is a free daily word puzzle, a Wordle-style game: guess the hidden five-letter word in six tries, with each guess marked to show which letters are in the right spot, in the wrong spot, or not in the word. There is one shared daily puzzle, so everyone gets the same word on the same calendar day, and a new one arrives at midnight in your own time zone.

Puzzles are numbered from #1 on 1 January 2026 and go up by one each day. A past puzzle moves into this list the day after it was live. "Play" opens that exact puzzle in your browser with the same word it had on its day; it will not change your streak in the app.

The free iPhone and iPad app adds day streaks, unlimited extra rounds, widgets and Game Center leaderboards, with no ads and no tracking.
